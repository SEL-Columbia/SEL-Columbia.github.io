<h3>The Rural Geospatial Settlement Assessment Tool</h3>
<p>This tool enables a fast transition from raw geospatial data to preliminary electrification network estimates. It includes: (a) a structure-merging module that converts satellite-predicted building footprints into estimated household locations; (b) an accelerated two-level network design algorithm, which generates artificial low- and medium-voltage distribution networks for given connection points. </p>
